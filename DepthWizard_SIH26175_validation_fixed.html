TECHNICAL DOCUMENTATION
SIH26175 Mission Report
A presentation-ready summary aligned to the supplied specification.

Organization: Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO)
Department: Department of Space / ISRO
Category: Software
Theme: Miscellaneous

Objective: Transform single-view optical RGB remote-sensing images into elevation maps and interactive 3D terrain.

Non-georeferenced: PNG/JPG → Relative DSM (rDSM).

Georeferenced: GeoTIFF → Absolute DSM with metric heights, using SRTM 30 m or limited GCPs for scale calibration.

Visualization: Project original optical imagery onto a generated terrain mesh and support first-person navigation plus structural-height and slope analysis.

Evaluation: RMSE, MAE and correlation against LiDAR/reference data; visualization quality, projection accuracy, fidelity, navigation, UX, stability and standalone deployment.

All numerical metrics in this prototype are illustrative UI values. They are not claimed measurements from the ISRO/SAC dataset.
